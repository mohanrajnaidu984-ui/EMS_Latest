Prepared for: Almoayyed Contracting Group Prepared by: Mohan Naidu Date: August 2026

Executive Summary

EMS (Enquiry Management System) is a custom full-stack enterprise platform managing the complete sales lifecycle — enquiry, pricing, quotation, approval, pipeline, and reporting. Built in-house by a single developer, it replaces a typical 12–24 month vendor project.

Recommended (employee): BHD 10,000–12,000 bonus + EMS Lead role  |  External replacement value: BHD 45,000–60,000
