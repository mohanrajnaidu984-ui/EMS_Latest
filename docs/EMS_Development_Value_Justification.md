## Executive Summary

EMS is a custom, full-stack enterprise platform that manages the complete sales and enquiry lifecycle for Almoayyed Contracting Group — from first customer contact through pricing, formal quotation, multi-level approval, pipeline tracking, and management reporting.

The system was designed, developed, and deployed **in-house by a single developer**, replacing what would typically require a **12–24 month external vendor project** with a multi-person team.

**Recommended recognition (employee scenario):** **BHD 10,000 – 12,000** one-time bonus, plus formal EMS Lead designation and separate annual maintenance terms if ongoing support is required.

**External replacement value (vendor benchmark):** **BHD 45,000 – 60,000** one-time.

## Business Value to Almoayyed Contracting Group

1. **Unified platform** — One system replaces scattered spreadsheets, email threads, and manual quote documents across divisions.
2. **Process control** — Structured enquiry → pricing → quote → approval flow reduces errors and enforces accountability.
3. **Management visibility** — Real-time dashboard, sales reports, and target tracking support forecasting and decision-making.
4. **Cost avoidance** — External development of equivalent scope in Bahrain typically costs **BHD 45,000 – 85,000+** and takes 12–24 months with a vendor team.
5. **Domain knowledge embedded** — Business rules (division logic, approval hierarchies, quote formats, SE assignments) are built into the system, not generic off-the-shelf software.
6. **Operational continuity** — The sole developer holds full system knowledge; retention reduces migration and support risk.
